# QA of the viewer, 2026-11-04

**Status:** open. A manual walk of the viewer, about 24 minutes, on the Vite development
server at commit `3ba8a417` in Chrome: the start page, a scenario's dialog, the viewer, a
replay, forks, stopping, resuming and deleting runs, and the new-run form. It looked at
how a person finds their way more than at the numbers: colours by field, the builder's
steps and the plant lab were left for another walk.

What it found is below. [The roadmap](README.md#after-a-qa) says what follows a QA.

Each was seen on the page, and D2 in the server's log as well.

## What a person should find

None of this is a defect. Each is planned, in this order.

**One way to watch a run.** *Replay* and *Follow* differ in no way a person can tell, so
one *Play* takes their place, in one interface: the replay's panel under the view, which
is what the walk liked best. The viewer's own play and follow go. Live steering has to
move first, since a replay's panels only fork for now, and the playback routes and the
policy's port are a separate question.

**A new run.** A scenario's dialog leads with starting a run and lists the runs under it,
and *Open in the viewer*, whose purpose was not clear, goes. *New run* takes the dialog's
place with a form: what the scenario is, its duration, its weather, its crops to read with a
way to make a new scenario from this one, and how fast to compute it. The seed is small
and optional. Empty, it is the scenario's own, as it is now, so that a run can be repeated,
and the form says so.

**The start page.** A title that says what the place is for, and no line under it. The two
choices equal, one under the other with "or" between them, each with an icon. Fewer words on
the page, and more in a tooltip on each button.

**The viewer's panels.** With a run open, no table of scenarios and no *Choose what to
show*: *Home* does that. A *Display* card beside *House*, with sections. Equipment reads
without identifiers, in groups. The version and not the commit, except on a branch. A name
that says it shows a run or a simulation.

**The panel under the view.** It folds, like the side panels, and their arrows take the
panel's whole height or width. Each button says what it does when the pointer is on it,
*Fork* first of all. *Other runs* goes, and a *Delete* takes its place.

**Buttons by what they do.** *Delete* in red and *Play* in green, each a colour of the
design system ([P19](p19-design-system.md)).

**The pace.** Whether "s a day" is a step of the simulation or a speed of showing it is not
clear from the page.

## Liked, and to keep

The *Contribute* button and its icon, which says code rather than money. The search for a
scenario, and its information. The panel under the view. The *Light panels* choice.
